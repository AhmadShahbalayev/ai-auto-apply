# Reusable answer drafts

Read USER.md and current preferences in applications.json first. Populate only from this person's real experience. These drafts are not submitted-answer history.

## Common form answers

Use confirmed contact information, location, availability, notice period and compensation preferences. Do not guess citizenship, work authorization, sponsorship or salary history. Skip optional unknowns and log required blockers.

## Career answers

Prepare short, natural drafts for an introduction, strongest achievement, challenging decision, relevant project/work example, collaboration and why a particular role fits. Include source/evidence and any limitations. A procurement profile may use supplier selection or process examples; an engineer may use technical delivery. Never reuse another candidate's stories.

## Writing style

Follow the “Natural, factual application answers” section in AGENTS.md. Draft from this person’s reviewed facts and voice; retain evidence and limitations beside each reusable story. Save the exact final questions/answers in that person's application record, without changing past submissions when drafts improve.
